Vicksburg Community Schools · Extracurricular Club

Building robots. Building engineers.

Vicksburg Robotics is the student-run extracurricular home of FRC Team 8126, the Vicksburg Control Freaks. Students design, build, and program competition robots — and take that engineering out into the community.

What we do

A season built on more than competition

Build & Compete

Every year the team designs, fabricates, and programs a robot for the FIRST Robotics Competition season — six weeks of build, then regional and district events.

Learn Real Skills

Programming, CAD, machining, electrical, project management, media, and business — students find a role that fits, on and off the robot.

Give Back

The team runs demos and outreach events for the Vicksburg community — schools, festivals, and local businesses — sharing what STEM and robotics can look like.
