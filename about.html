About the club

Who we are

Vicksburg Robotics is Vicksburg Community Schools' extracurricular robotics club, separate from the district's in-class CTE engineering courses. Students from Vicksburg Middle and High School build FRC Team 8126, the Vicksburg Control Freaks — designing, building, and programming a competition robot each season, and taking their engineering skills out into hands-on community events.

The team

What students do on the team

Team roles aren't just "who builds the robot." Every season needs programmers, fabricators, storytellers, and documentarians working together.

Programmer

Writes and tests the robot's Java code — autonomous routines, driver controls, and sensor/vision integration.

Builder / Fabrication

CAD design, CNC and manual machining, wiring, and mechanical assembly of the robot.

Media

Photography, video, and social media documenting builds, competitions, and outreach.

Engineer's Portfolio

Documents the engineering design process for judged awards and team records.

In the community

Outreach

Beyond competition, the team demos its robots and shares STEM at community events around Vicksburg — school visits, local festivals, and industry partner events — showing what hands-on engineering looks like.
